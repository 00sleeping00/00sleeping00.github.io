The Language of Flowers

An interactive work in two parts. Begin with the wind, then open the book.

PART I

The Wind Keeps a Garden

A photograph, or your camera, dissolves into drifting petals. Open your palm to scatter them; close your fist to gather them into a flower.

To use your hands, select “Enable camera” and allow camera access. Without a camera, the mouse, buttons and keys 0–9 play it too.
