IV · The Moonlit Maze

Quiet Luxury

A quiet, light living room by day, walked through in Unity.

Role
Solo work
Type
3D interior scene · Unity (URP) · 1 min 10 s

At a glance

  1. i.Ivory upholstery with warm wood and charcoal accents keeps the room quiet and understated.
  2. ii.Daylight through clear glazing meets warm lamps; contact shadows anchor the rug and furniture to the honed stone floor.
  3. iii.A slim wall-mounted screen, a low timber cabinet and a narrow console keep the room uncluttered.

Notes

Subtle normal maps bring out the weave of the fabric, while low gloss keeps the upholstery soft in daylight. Balanced exposure keeps detail in both the bright window and the darker furniture as the camera moves through the room.
